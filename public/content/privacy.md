# Privacy policy for Daily Nomie

## Your data stays on your device
Daily Nomie stores your logs, trackers, goals, experiments and settings in your browser. Nothing is sent to us, because we run no server that receives your data.

## Sync (optional)
If you set up sync, your data is copied to a CouchDB server that you choose and run. We do not run that server.

## Backups
A backup is a file you download. It contains your data and most settings. It never contains your PIN, your storage type, or your AI keys.

## AI (optional, off by default)
When you turn AI on and add your own key, the features you use send the context needed for that request to the provider you chose:
- **Anthropic (Claude)**
- **OpenRouter**, which routes requests to other AI models. Its free models may be run by providers that keep logs of prompts.

Your API key is kept on your device. You can turn AI off at any time in Settings.

## Analytics
Daily Nomie does not load any analytics or tracking scripts.

## Changes to this policy
We announce changes to this policy in the What's New section of the app.
